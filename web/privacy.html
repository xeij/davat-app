privacy

Davat is for sending invites and answering them. We keep only what that needs.

what we store when you answer an invite

  • the name you type
  • your answer: going, maybe or can't
  • chat messages you post on the invite
  • whether you said you paid, if the invite collects money

Your browser also keeps a random id for the invite so you can change your answer later. Clearing your browser data removes it.

what we don't do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We don't sell or share your data. Payments happen in Venmo or Cash App, and we never see them.

who sees what

Anyone with the invite link sees the guest list (names and answers), the money raised so far, and the chat. Only the host sees who said they paid.

deleting your info

When a host deletes an invite, its answers and chat go with it. To remove something sooner, write to us.
